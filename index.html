About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Houston. My research lies at the intersection of inverse problems, scientific machine learning, learning dynamical systems, and numerical analysis.

Before joining the University of Houston in 2024, I was an Assistant Professor at Illinois Institute of Technology, an Assistant Research Scientist at the Texas A&M Institute of Data Science, and a postdoctoral researcher at Johns Hopkins University. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Maryland.

I am a co-principal investigator on the NSF project A Bayesian Paradigm for Physics-Informed Machine Learning and received a Ralph E. Powe Junior Faculty Enhancement Award for data-driven modeling of emergent behavior in social science.

Research at a glance

01

Learning governing dynamics

Recovering low-dimensional laws from high-dimensional observations of interacting and stochastic systems.

02

Partially observed systems

Identifying hidden states and parameters when scientific observations are incomplete or indirect.

03

Computational foundations

Building stable, interpretable, and efficient numerical methods for scientific machine learning.
